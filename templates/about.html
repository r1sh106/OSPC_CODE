His journey

Born on 21 July 2000 in Leeds, where his father Alf-Inge played professionally, Haaland grew up in Bryne, Norway. He developed at Bryne and Molde under Ole Gunnar Solskjaer, announced himself at Red Bull Salzburg, then took Germany by storm at Borussia Dortmund. In 2022 he joined Manchester City.

2022-23: the treble

In his first season at City he won the Champions League, Premier League and FA Cup.

  • Champions League top scorer: 12 goals in City's title run.
  • Premier League record: 36 goals in 35 games, the most in a 38-game season.
  • Norway: the country's all-time leading international scorer.
